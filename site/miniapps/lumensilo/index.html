Understanding DLI & Energy Metrics in Vertical Farming

Daily Light Integral (DLI) measures the cumulative amount of active photons delivered to a specific square meter over 24 hours. Matching this to crop-specific biological parameters is critical to maximize yields and minimize energy overheads.

1. The Role of Photoperiod

Extending the photoperiod (hours per day) allows you to achieve target DLI goals even when using lower-powered, more economical LED fixtures. This prevents the leaf damage often associated with high-intensity spikes while minimizing heat generation.

2. Optimizing Operating Expenses (OPEX)

Artificial lighting can represent up to 60% of an indoor farm's total energy bill. Protesting and modeling fixture counts, shelf layouts, and wattage loads with LumenSilo before installation helps urban growers structure highly profitable agricultural systems.
