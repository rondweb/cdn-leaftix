04 // VPD Deep-Dive

VPD in practice: 22.4°C · 68% RH → 0.92 kPa

Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) is the drying power of the air around your leaf. At 22.4°C and 68% relative humidity, BioStick reads a VPD of 0.92 kPa — right in the photosynthesis sweet spot. Staying there means transpiration flows, stomata stay open, and you irrigate only when the plant actually needs it — up to 30% less water.
